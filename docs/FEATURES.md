# KAMI — Beyond Reality

Clean-start Windows-only Second Life viewer project. Native DirectX 12 world renderer; no OpenGL or Black Dragon code. This is a requirements document, not a claim that the viewer is built.

## World
Second Life login, CAPS and event queue, region/parcel terrain, water, sky, objects, avatars, attachments, inventory, mesh, animations, teleport, messaging and PBR textures; respect Second Life protocol and upload limits.

## Graphics
1440p default, optional 4K/8K, adaptive quality, potato/performance modes, volumetric lighting and fog, SSR, ambient occlusion, tone mapping, FXAA/TAA, PBR controls, DLSS/FSR/XeSS, frame generation and optical flow when supported. No bloom, MXAO/GShade or legacy avatar lighting.

## Interface and camera
Original KAMI liquid-glass-inspired UI (not an Apple clone), light/dark themes, Japanese Fire Garden login, accessibility, World button and live graphics preview. WASD/arrows and original camera with 75-degree FOV target, 50% sensitivity, 15% smoothing, 4m follow distance, collision, recenter, head tracking and no shake.

## Creation and social
Creator Studio, Photo Studio, mesh uploads, Smart Sort by store/creator (including bracketed names), Area Search, Follow Avatar, Teleport To, viewer-used indicator where supported, Kami Alive messaging and KAMI-to-KAMI read receipts.

## Delivery
Windows KAMIViewer.exe; closed testing, then public beta; hotfixes and push notifications; approximately 500 MB distribution target. The owner controls releases. No demo shell should be presented as a completed viewer.

## Separate initiative
Unreal Engine 5 KAMI viewer remains a separate project.
